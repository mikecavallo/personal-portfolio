About me

I am a self-taught programmer with a passion for technology that started at the age of 12. As the book market shifted and industry dynamics changed, I saw an opportunity to pivot once again—this time toward my true passion: Artificial Intelligence. Over the last two years, I’ve been dedicated to building AI applications and deepening my expertise in AI-driven software development and machine learning. I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and pushing the boundaries of what’s possible with AI & ML.

Whether it's developing intelligent systems, exploring machine learning frameworks, or integrating AI into real-world applications, I thrive on innovation and problem-solving. This portfolio showcases my journey, projects, and the exciting work I’m creating in the web development space.
