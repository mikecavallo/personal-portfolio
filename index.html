About me

I'm Mike, an AI developer and consultant in Leland, North Carolina. I've been building software for over 20 years: websites, web apps, servers and the integrations that tie them together.

These days I build practical AI for small businesses: voice agents that answer the phone, automations that handle follow-ups and scheduling, and custom AI tools for the repetitive work that eats a week. Running my own e-commerce business taught me where the hours actually go, and that is what I build for.

My client work runs through Business Runs Better. This page is my personal profile and a place for side projects.
